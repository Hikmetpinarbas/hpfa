# HPFA Analyst Visualization Policy V1

Status: CURRENT PRESENTATION GOVERNANCE  
Role: analyst-facing visualization policy; not runtime football truth  
Authority: ZFGV evidence/claim contracts remain upstream authority.

## Purpose

HPFA graphics exist to compress admitted football evidence for an analyst. A chart is a presentation surface: it cannot create evidence, repair missing semantics, increase independence, widen a claim ceiling or turn a proxy into physical/tactical truth.

Canonical rendering chain:

SOURCE → ADMITTED OBSERVATION → GOVERNED FEATURE/FINDING → VIEW MODEL → VISUALIZATION

The renderer consumes governed artifacts. It does not infer a stronger football meaning than the artifact already carries.

## Visual truth locks

- action coordinate != player position;
- coordinate != tracking;
- event/action rate != possession or dominance;
- same timestamp != ordered sequence;
- provider label != physical/tactical truth;
- aggregate metric != action identity;
- model value != fact;
- visual prominence != confidence;
- missing/unknown observations are not rendered as zero;
- no chart may silently merge dependent reflections as independent support.
## Admitted professional visual families

When upstream semantics permit them, HPFA may render: admitted action-location maps; shot-location maps; visible loss/recovery maps; final-third/box-access observations; visible process and consequence timelines; six-phase/process-family distributions; game-state exposure profiles; pass/carry/action-family mixes; goalkeeper distribution observations; player process-participation timelines; recurrence/variant/deviation comparisons; evidence/counterevidence cards; and numerator/eligible-denominator tables.

Names must describe what is actually observed. Prefer labels such as “median action location”, “visible recovery location”, “shot-linked process”, “event-derived progression proxy”, or “provider-labelled aerial duel outcome”.

## Constructs requiring stronger observation

Without admitted tracking/video or an equivalent direct observation surface, do not label a visualization as true team shape, compactness, line height, pitch control, pressure geometry, off-ball structure, true player/ball speed, physical load, body orientation, scanning, coach intention, tactical dominance or causality.

Historical event-derived versions of these ideas may be studied only after renaming them to the observable construct they actually measure. A proxy never inherits the physical/tactical construct name.

## Coordinate and direction contract

A pitch plot requires admitted coordinate frame, scale, axis meaning and team direction. No fixed 105x68, 100x50 or left-to-right assumption becomes product truth merely because a historical source used it.

If direction is unresolved, render source-frame coordinates or downgrade/withhold directional claims. Do not infer direction from a convenient visual pattern and then treat that inference as source truth.
## Quantitative rendering contract

Rates should expose numerator and eligible denominator where available. Small denominators remain visible. Percentage formatting must not imply evidential strength.

Every model-derived value displayed in a professional output must retain model/source/version/admission status when material to interpretation. xG, xT, VAEP, OBV or similar values remain model outputs unless the exact source contract says otherwise.

Time-window comparisons must expose the window and observation basis. Arbitrary 5/10/15-second or minute windows are analytical parameters, not universal football constants.

## Design standard

HPFA visuals use restrained analyst-facing design. Avoid esports/neon dashboards, decorative gauges, arbitrary composite scores, traffic-light certainty theatre, 3D decoration, excessive badges, and chart junk.

Color, size, opacity and ordering must encode declared variables only. Unknown, unresolved, review-required and censored states must be visually distinguishable from negative/zero outcomes.

A report should prioritize: what was observed; where/when; consequence; recurrence/variant; counterevidence; safe meaning; and what cannot be inferred.

## Visualization acceptance

A professional visual is admissible only when its upstream artifact is current-run bound where required, its labels match the construct ceiling, dependent evidence is not multiplied, missingness is preserved, denominator/model context is available when needed, and a reviewer can trace it back to machine-readable evidence.

Historical Graphics Research Pack V1 supplied useful visualization ideas, but its event-only framing and several physical/tactical proxy names are not current authority. The useful concepts are rehabilitated here under ZFGV; the raw pack is historical support only.
