# HPFA Brand Identity Core V1

Status: `SPEC_ONLY`

Purpose: define HPFA brand language, positioning, and visual direction.

This document is a governance and communication artifact. It does not change runtime code, ACTIVE_MATCH execution, schemas, claim gates, or tests.

---

## Brand Definition

HPFA is not a football statistics site.

HPFA is an event-only football intelligence system. It converts row-level match evidence into structured, auditable, claim-safe analysis.

The brand should feel rigorous, architectural, and reliable. It should not feel like sports entertainment, meme content, or a gamified dashboard.

---

## Core Traits

1. Rigorous: every analytical statement is tied to evidence.
2. Systemic: observations are connected through a chain, not presented as isolated notes.
3. Quiet Authority: clear, firm, controlled language.
4. Intellectual Depth: football is treated as an information problem.
5. Epistemic Honesty: uncertainty, missing evidence, and blocked claims are stated openly.

---

## Tone of Voice

HPFA language should be concise, declarative, and evidence-bound.

Preferred language:

- visible event surface indicates
- row-level evidence shows
- action-family volume suggests
- candidate signal detected
- requires later validation
- blocked by claim boundary

Avoid:

- shocking
- unbelievable
- crazy stat
- domination language without evidence gate
- coach or player intention language
- entertainment-first phrasing

---

## Positioning

HPFA converts football event data from raw observation into structured football intelligence.

HPFA classifies analytical statements by evidence level, context, uncertainty, and falsifiability. It does not present isolated interesting findings as finished truth. It builds explainable and repeatable analytical outputs.

HPFA maps what can be known from event data, what can only be inferred, and what must remain blocked.

---

## Visual Direction

The visual identity should communicate:

- precision
- architecture
- evidence
- depth
- rigor

Preferred metaphors:

- directed graph
- evidence chain
- layered system
- node-link structure
- raw signal transformed into structured intelligence

The logo should communicate: raw data to structured intelligence.

---

## Styles to Avoid

Avoid:

- neon esports dashboard style
- loud gradients
- meme graphics
- football ball plus green pitch cliché
- tabloid headline language
- gamified badges or levels
- generic stock football images

## Product Alignment

Brand language must stay aligned with HPFA claim safety.

Safe phrasing:

- visible surface evidence indicates
- row-level evidence shows
- sequence candidate detected
- pattern support surface appears
- evidence is insufficient for that claim

Blocked phrasing:

- coach intended
- team dominated
- opponent was tactically broken
- off-ball structure shows
- team controlled space
- fatigue caused

---

## Brand Promise

HPFA turns visible football evidence into disciplined football intelligence.

It shows what happened, where it happened, in which context it happened, what evidence supports it, which claim ceiling applies, and which statements must remain blocked.

HPFA's strength is not overclaiming from data. HPFA's strength is knowing how far the data can safely go.
